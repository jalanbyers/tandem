## Emergency fund guideline

The standard guideline is **3–6 months of essential expenses**. A starting target of
**$11,700 (3 months)** means saving about **$550/month for 12 months**.

Building the emergency fund before other goals is foundational: it prevents forced
withdrawals from retirement accounts during shocks.
