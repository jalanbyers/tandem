# Time in the market vs timing the market

> Fictional education page in the style of a brokerage market-education library.
> Educational content, not individualized investment advice.

Market drops feel urgent, but reacting to them is historically expensive: missing just the
**10 best market days over 20 years cut returns roughly in half** in historical studies.
The best days tend to cluster near the worst ones, which is why selling into a decline so
often locks in losses.

## What a drop means for a long horizon

For a retirement horizon measured in decades, a single week's move rarely changes the
projection meaningfully. Plans built with scenario simulation already *expect* weeks like
this one.

## When to talk to a human

Deciding whether to sell — especially under stress — deserves a conversation with a
licensed professional who can weigh your full situation. That is a strength of a plan,
not a failure of nerve.
