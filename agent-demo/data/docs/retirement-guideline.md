# Retirement savings guideline — how much is "on track"?

> Fictional education page in the style of Fidelity guidance. Educational content, not
> individualized investment advice.

## The 10x rule of thumb

Aim to save **10x your salary by age 67**. Milestones along the way: 1x by 30, 3x by 40,
6x by 50, 8x by 60, 10x by 67.

These are guidelines, not guarantees — they assume steady contributions, an age-based
asset mix, and retirement at 67. Adequacy is usually expressed as **income replacement**:
the share of pre-retirement income your savings can sustainably replace.

## Methodology

Projections here use the 10x-by-67 guideline with a 4-scenario market simulation.
Assumptions are shown on request. Projections are estimates only.

## The employer match

Before any other optimization, capture the full employer match — it is an immediate,
guaranteed return on contributions and the single most common unclaimed benefit in
workplace plans.
