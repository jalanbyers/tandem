# Session 0182 — same client calling back a second time in eight days

> Fictional. No real advisor, client, firm, or account.
>
> The repeat call is the interesting part. The move under study is what the advisor does
> when reassurance has already been given once and evidently did not hold.

[t01] **CLI:** It's me again. I know you already told me all this last week.

[t02] **ADV:** You don't need to apologise for calling twice. If the first conversation had actually settled it you wouldn't be ringing, so something didn't land — and that's useful information for me, not an imposition.

[t03] **CLI:** I did understand what you said. I just don't *believe* it at 7am.

[t04] **ADV:** That's a really precise description and it's more common than you'd think. Understanding and believing are different systems, and mine only talks to the first one.

[t05] **CLI:** So what do I do?

[t06] **ADV:** Can I ask what specifically happens at 7am? Is it the app, is it the news, is it a conversation?

[t07] **CLI:** I check the balance before I get out of bed.

[t08] **ADV:** Then I'd stop trying to win the argument with better numbers, because I gave you the numbers and they lasted about four days. The thing we should change is the 7am part, not your understanding of volatility.

[t09] **CLI:** That feels like avoiding it.

[t10] **ADV:** Here's the honest tradeoff. Checking daily costs you a small amount of distress every single morning, compounded, and it buys you no information whatsoever — nothing you do at 7am changes anything by 8. Not checking costs you the feeling of vigilance, which is genuinely hard to give up, and buys you your mornings back.

[t11] **CLI:** When you say it like that.

[t12] **ADV:** I'm not asking you to be zen about it. I'm asking you to move the check to Friday.

[t13] **CLI:** I could do Friday.

[t14] **ADV:** Right. So this time we're not changing the portfolio and we're not re-running the projection — we tried that and it didn't hold for you. We're changing when you look. Friday, and we'll speak in a fortnight either way.

[t15] **CLI:** Okay. Yes.

[t16] **ADV:** And if Friday doesn't hold either, ring me a third time. I'd genuinely rather that than you quietly selling at 7am one morning.
