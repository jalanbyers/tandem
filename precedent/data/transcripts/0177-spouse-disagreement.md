# Session 0177 — two decision-makers who disagree

[t05] **ADV:** Let me make sure I've got each of you. You're carrying the risk of doing nothing and it's costing you something to sit with. And you're carrying the memory of previous moves not working out, so action itself feels like the risk.

[t07] **CLI-B:** That's about right.

[t08] **ADV:** Then you're not actually arguing about the portfolio. You're each protecting against the thing that hurt last time, and those are different things. That's worth naming because it's not resolvable with a chart.
